Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to BIMSCOPE ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our website and IFC Viewer application.

By using BIMSCOPE, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of our services.

2. Information We Collect

BIMSCOPE is designed with a privacy-first approach. We collect minimal information:

2.1 Information You Provide

  • IFC Files: Files you load into the viewer are processed entirely in your browser. We do not upload, store, or transmit your IFC files to any server.
  • Metadata Files: Optional JSON metadata files are also processed locally in your browser and are never sent to our servers.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Language Preference: We store your selected language (English or Thai) in your browser's localStorage and optionally in a cookie if you consent.
  • Cookie Consent: We store your cookie consent preference in a browser cookie.

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Payment or financial information
  • IP addresses or device fingerprints
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Your IFC model data or building information

3. How We Use Information

The limited information we collect is used solely for:

  • Language Preference: To display the website in your preferred language across sessions.
  • Cookie Consent: To remember whether you have accepted or declined cookies.
  • Application Functionality: To provide the IFC viewing, inspection, and export features entirely within your browser.

4. Cookies

We use only essential cookies:

Cookie Name Purpose Duration
bimscope_cookie_consent Stores your cookie consent choice 30–365 days
bimscope_language Stores your language preference 365 days

We do not use any third-party tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics cookies.

5. Data Storage & Security

All IFC file processing occurs entirely within your web browser using WebAssembly technology. Your building data never leaves your device. We do not operate any backend servers that receive or store your data.

Language preferences are stored locally in your browser's localStorage and cookies. This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to us.

6. Third-Party Services

BIMSCOPE uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts: We load fonts (Inter, Noto Sans Thai, JetBrains Mono) from Google Fonts. Google may collect basic connection information as described in their privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages / Hosting: Our website may be hosted on a third-party platform that may collect standard server logs.

We do not use any analytics services (such as Google Analytics), advertising networks, or social media tracking pixels.

7. Children's Privacy

BIMSCOPE is a professional tool intended for architects, engineers, and BIM professionals. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age.

8. Your Rights

Since we collect minimal data that is stored only on your device, you have full control:

  • Delete cookies: Clear your browser cookies at any time to remove stored preferences.
  • Clear localStorage: Use your browser's developer tools to clear localStorage data.
  • Decline cookies: You can decline cookies when prompted, and the site will still function normally.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our website.
